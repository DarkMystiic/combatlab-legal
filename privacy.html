COMBATLAB

Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

CombatLab is made by Jaylen. This policy explains what data the app uses. The short version: we don't ask for an account, we don't show ads, we don't track you, and your training data stays on your device.

Data that stays on your device

Your settings (style, stance, level, voice, timer choices), your workout history (date, rounds, minutes and number of combos), your streak and the combos you build in My Combos are saved only on your phone or tablet.

We never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details.

To know which plan you have, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history from the store, and basic technical data such as device type, operating system, app version, country and IP address. It does not receive your name or email from us. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

No account, name, email or phone number. No location. No contacts, photos or microphone. No advertising identifiers, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Children

CombatLab is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Younger athletes should train with a parent or coach.

Your choices and rights

You can delete all on-device data by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

For anything about purchase data held by RevenueCat, or any privacy question, contact us at jaylendgotico@gmail.com and we will help, including access or deletion requests under laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

Changes

If the app starts collecting new data (for example accounts or gym leaderboards), we will update this policy before that feature ships and change the date above.
